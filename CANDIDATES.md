# Candidates — Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting (u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-cat-voicemail-greeting-8eaad443)

Five lines were drafted for this bounty; the most obvious one was thrown out before the winner was picked.

- A (DISCARDED first idea): "You've reached Whiskers. I'm napping. Leave a message after the beep — or just bring tuna." — the most obvious cat-voicemail line; punchline is just "bring tuna".
- B: Chairman Meow knocking a water glass off, "I'll ignore it at 4 a.m." — good 4 a.m. twist, but "Chairman Meow" is a familiar pun name and the brief punishes familiar puns.
- D: Pickles: "the beep is me" — surprising twist, but the middle ("the human is asleep, I'm in charge") is a generic take-over trope with no second cat-specific beat.
- E: Professor Whiskers: "Meow your name, your crime, and why my food bowl is still empty." — strong bureaucrat-cat voice, but the name is a pun and the punchline is a list, not a turn.
- F: Tuna judging from the top of the fridge, "I'll knock it off later" — good double meaning, but "knock it off" is a stock phrase and the setup has no self-own.
- C (CHOSEN): "You've reached Biscuit. I can't come to the phone — I'm stuck in a box I chose. Leave a message; I'll sit on it later." (118 characters)

## Why C

- It combines a self-own with two behaviours only a cat would show — getting stuck in a box of its own choosing, and sitting on everything, your message included — plus a punchline that turns on a double meaning rather than a ready-made phrase.
- Biscuit comes across as a distinct character: smug, unbothered, and wholly responsible for the mess he is in. He never apologises; he just passes the job along.
- The twist arrives with the closing words ("I'll sit on it later"). Setup:punchline ratio about 2.4:1.
- Originality checks: an exact-phrase web search returned 0 verbatim hits, and the leaderboard was empty at build time (blind rounds), so there was no rival text to scan.
